TRADE SCHEME FX — ALL-IN-ONE MARKET VISUAL UPGRADE

Included:
- Existing Trade Scheme FX Deriv OAuth project bundle.
- Global scrolling ticker visible at the top of every page.
- Markets page at /markets with forex, metals, crypto, indices and Deriv synthetic categories.
- Server-side quote endpoint at /api/markets.
- Twelve Data integration for EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, XAU/USD and XAG/USD.

REAL DATA STATUS
The ticker and market page are implemented. Real prices require a valid Twelve Data API key and provider-plan support for each symbol. Without a key, the website clearly shows FEED OFF / DATA FEED NOT CONNECTED. No fake prices are displayed.
Crypto, indices and Deriv synthetic cards are category placeholders until separate compatible data feeds are connected. Deriv synthetic indices need an authorized Deriv market-data connection. No trading execution is implemented.

DEPLOY
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload/merge the contents of tsfx-deriv-oauth into the existing GitHub repository, preserving folders.
3. Commit changes and wait for Vercel deployment.
4. Vercel > Project > Settings > Environment Variables: add TWELVEDATA_API_KEY from your Twelve Data account.
5. Redeploy and open https://tradesche.com/markets. The scrolling ticker appears globally.

SECURITY
Keep TWELVEDATA_API_KEY server-side; do not use NEXT_PUBLIC_ and do not paste the key into public source or chat.
Keep DERIV_OAUTH_ENABLED disabled until Deriv approval and the previously discussed encryption-key rotation are complete.
